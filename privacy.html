Privacy Policy

Applies to the Top Spot Race YouTube channel and to Top Spot Race Publisher. Last updated: 28 September 2026.

What Top Spot Race Publisher is

Top Spot Race Publisher is a private desktop application used only by the owner of the Top Spot Race YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos and to organise them into the channel's playlists. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, other people.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth, scopes youtube.upload and youtube), the application:

  • uploads videos to the Top Spot Race channel and sets their title, description, tags, category and visibility;
  • adds those videos to the channel's own playlists;
  • reads information about the channel's own videos and playlists (for example their IDs and public view, like and comment counts) to check that uploads worked and to measure how our videos perform.

We do not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or of any other YouTube user.

How we store it

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the channel owner's computer. A record of our own uploads (video ID, title, publish date and public statistics) is kept in a private database used only by us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from our computer; upload records are deleted on request within 30 days.

YouTube and Google

Top Spot Race Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers of our videos

Watching our videos on YouTube is covered by YouTube's own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We only see the aggregated, anonymous statistics YouTube shows every channel owner.
